# Awesome App Icon Resources

A short, curated list of official guidelines, galleries and tools for designing and shipping app icons for iOS, Android and the web.

Disclosure: this list is maintained by the developer of APPLORE, which is listed below. Suggestions for other good resources are welcome via pull request.

## Official guidelines

- [Apple Human Interface Guidelines: App icons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons) - Apple's rules for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS and visionOS icons, including dark and tinted variants.
- [Icon Composer](https://developer.apple.com/icon-composer/) - Apple's tool for layered icons with the Liquid Glass look.
- [Android adaptive icons](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/launch/icon_design_adaptive) - Foreground, background and monochrome layers, sizes and safe zone.
- [Google Play icon design specifications](https://developer.android.com/distribute/google-play/resources/icon-design-specifications) - The 512 × 512 px Play Store icon rules.
- [Material Design 3: Icons](https://m3.material.io/styles/icons/overview) - Google's guidance for system and product icons.

## Inspiration and galleries

- [APPLORE](https://applore.app/) - Archive of 18,000+ real App Store and Google Play icons, browsable by color, style and category, with every past version of each icon.
- [iOS Icon Gallery](https://www.iosicongallery.com) - Long-running curated gallery of iOS app icons.
- [macOS Icons](https://macosicons.com) - Community collection of macOS app icons.

## Symbols

- [SF Symbols](https://developer.apple.com/sf-symbols/) - Apple's symbol library for iOS and macOS interfaces.
- [Material Symbols](https://fonts.google.com/icons) - Google's open-source symbol set.

## Generators and exporters

- [APPLORE app icon generator](https://applore.app/app-icon-generator) - One 1024 px image to every iOS, macOS, Android (adaptive and themed) and web size, in the browser, free and without an account.
- [IconKitchen](https://icon.kitchen) - Android and iOS launcher icons from clip art, text or an image.
- [App Icon Generator (appicon.co)](https://www.appicon.co) - Exports iOS and Android icon sets from one image.
- [MakeAppIcon](https://makeappicon.com) - Icon set exporter for iOS and Android.

## AI and animation

- [APPLORE AI app icon generator](https://applore.app/ai-app-icon-generator) - Generates an icon for your app using a real App Store icon or a style as the reference.
- [APPLORE logo animation](https://applore.app/logo-animation) - Animates a logo or app icon and exports Lottie JSON or animated SVG for splash screens.

## Preview

- [APPLORE app icon preview](https://applore.app/app-icon-preview) - See an icon on iPhone and Android home screens and store pages before you ship.

## Reading

- [App icon sizes for iOS, Android and the web](https://applore.app/blog/app-icon-sizes) - Every size you need in 2026, with sources.
- [App Icon Colors: What 18,410 Real Apps Actually Use](https://applore.app/blog/app-icon-colors) - Color data from the App Store and Google Play.
